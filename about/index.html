About the project

Cockerh.am is a genealogy hub for the Cockerham surname, run by James Cockerham of Lexington, Kentucky.

The goal is simple: gather what can actually be documented about Cockerham families, starting close to home in Kentucky, and share it in one place so relatives and fellow researchers can build on it.

How it works

  • Sources first. Facts go up when there's a record behind them. Leads and guesses are labeled as such.
  • Open and correctable. Anyone can suggest additions or fixes through GitHub issues, and every change to the site is tracked.
  • Privacy for the living. Details about living people aren't published without their permission.

This is early-stage research. Nothing here should be treated as final until it's backed by a cited source.
