Resources

Good places to start researching Kentucky families. Most are free.

Records & trees

  • FamilySearch: free records and a shared world tree.
  • Find a Grave: cemetery and burial records with photos.
  • U.S. National Archives: Genealogy: census, military and immigration records.
  • Chronicling America: historic U.S. newspapers from the Library of Congress.

Kentucky

  • Kentucky Department for Libraries and Archives: state records, vital records and county materials.
  • Kentucky Historical Society: library, collections and genealogy help.
  • Kentucky Genealogical Society.
  • Kentucky Digital Newspaper Program (University of Kentucky).
  • KYGenWeb: volunteer county-by-county resources.
