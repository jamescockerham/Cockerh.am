Contribute or get in touch

Contributions come in through GitHub issues, so every submission has a public, trackable record. A free GitHub account is all you need.

  • Add a person or branch
  • Share a photo
  • Share a document or source
  • Share DNA results
  • Report a correction

Before you post

  • Issues are public. Don't post private details about living people, such as addresses, phone numbers or birth dates.
  • Include your source when you can: a record, a book, a gravestone, a family Bible.
  • Only share photos and documents you have the right to share.
