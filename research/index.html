Research in progress

The current focus is the Cockerham family's roots in Kentucky.

Where things stand

The research is in its early stages. Records are being gathered and checked, and nothing has been published as a family tree yet, so you won't find unsourced names or dates here.

What's being worked on

  • Tracing Cockerham lines in Kentucky through census, vital and county records.
  • Collecting family photos, documents and stories, with permission.
  • Connecting with other Cockerham researchers and relatives.

Can you help?

If you have Cockerham records, photos, family stories, or DNA matches, especially with Kentucky connections, please get in touch.

A family-history project by James Cockerham, Lexington, Kentucky. Research in progress: please verify before you copy.
